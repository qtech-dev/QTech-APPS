Summary

  • No account. The Apps do not ask you to create an account or give an email address or password.
  • Progress is stored on your device. Your answers, history, and settings are stored locally. Scores and study events also go to Amplitude, and what you send to an AI feature goes to OpenAI. A first name, if you enter one, stays on your device unless you choose to share something that shows it.
  • App and website analytics differ. In the Apps, Amplitude receives usage and study events tied to pseudonymous identifiers, including your exam date and any exam result you report. See Section 3. Optional website analytics starts only after you allow it and includes practice answers, correctness, scores and timing; see Section 7.
  • AI features use OpenAI. What you send to an AI feature is stored in OpenAI's API logs and may be reviewed by us. See Section 5.
  • Apple handles payment. We never receive your card details.
  • No ads, and no selling data for money. The Apps show no ads, and we do not sell personal information for money. Part 107 FAA Practice Test sends information to Google to measure our advertising, which some laws may treat as a sale or sharing; see Section 6.

2. Information stored on your device

Your first name, study history, plan, and settings are stored in the App's storage on your device. Deleting an App removes that storage, with two exceptions. The random device ID is kept in your device's keychain and can survive deleting the App. Backups of your device made through iCloud or a computer may also contain App data. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we do not receive them.

Your name can appear on certificates and result screens. Apart from device backups, it leaves your device only if you share an item showing it. App reminders are scheduled on your device with your permission. They are not sent from our servers, and you can turn them off in iOS Settings.

3. Analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how the Apps are used, measure whether our study features work, and improve them. Amplitude receives the events and properties described in Section 1, linked to your IDFV and random app user ID. That data is pseudonymous: it does not contain your name or email address, but it is connected to an identifier and is not anonymous. We also copy the app user ID to RevenueCat, so analytics and purchase records for an App installation can be connected.

Current App versions do not offer an in-app switch to turn analytics off. You can stop further collection by deleting the App, and you can ask us about existing data using the rights process in Section 12.

5. AI features and conversation logs

AI features are optional. When you use one, the content listed under "AI requests" in Section 1 is sent through our backend, hosted by Vercel, to OpenAI to generate a reply. Anything personal that you include becomes part of that request.

Storage and review. We turn on storage for these requests and replies in OpenAI's API logs. Authorized people working on our Apps may review them to troubleshoot problems, provide support, evaluate answer quality, improve the tutor, and investigate misuse. We do not use tutor conversations to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell them.

What OpenAI receives. We do not add your device ID, app user ID, or IP address to the content we send to OpenAI. Because stored conversations do not carry those identifiers, we can usually find one only by App name, time, and content.

Retention and training. OpenAI's documentation states that stored API responses are kept for at least 30 days, that abuse-monitoring logs are kept for up to 30 days unless longer retention is required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent harm, and that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in. See OpenAI's API data controls for its current terms.

Your part. Do not submit passwords, payment details, identifiable patient or client information, or other sensitive information about yourself or anyone else. Deleting a conversation on your device or deleting the App does not delete copies already held by our service providers.

6. Advertising and attribution

The Apps show no ads and do not show Apple's tracking prompt, so iOS does not give them a usable advertising identifier (IDFA). We advertise the Apps on other platforms, and those platforms handle your data under their own policies when you see or click an ad there.

Most Apps. Most App versions use Apple's SKAdNetwork system, and Apple sends its install and subscription-conversion reports to our attribution provider, Tenjin. Those reports do not identify you. Apart from the App described below, current Apps contain no advertising SDK. Some older App versions may have included Tenjin's SDK and shared your IDFV, a zeroed advertising identifier, and purchase events with Tenjin for the same purpose.

Part 107 FAA Practice Test. This App also uses Google's Firebase Analytics to measure installs and App activity, including for our Google Ads campaigns. Google receives identifiers such as an app-instance ID and IDFV, usage events, device data, and your IP address, and handles them under its own policy. This App also sends Apple's AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat so that we can connect installs and purchases to campaigns. To stop this collection, delete the App. You can also contact us using Section 12.

7. Our websites

neurontrainer.com is hosted by Vercel and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views. If you allow optional analytics, we also use Amplitude to understand page visits, exam discovery, practice results, App Store link clicks, contact-link clicks and page performance. Practice analytics include the question and topic, available answer choices, your selected answer, the correct answer, whether your answer was correct, answer changes before checking, time spent, and scores for fully observed practice attempts. These details help us identify confusing questions and improve study tools; sample-quiz scores are not predictions of professional exam performance. Events also include the public page path, browser and device information, a random browser identifier, session information and limited traffic-source information. They do not include form contents, email contents, full page URLs or unrecognized search text. Search reporting uses recognized exam/category labels and result counts. A contact-link click does not tell us whether you sent an email, and an App Store click does not tell us whether you installed an app.

Optional Amplitude analytics remain off until you choose to allow them. Use “Analytics settings” in the footer to change your choice. We honor browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by keeping this optional collection off. Your choice is kept in local storage for up to 180 days and, when allowed, a random analytics identifier for up to 30 days; session metadata expires after 30 minutes of inactivity and is kept in session storage. Turning analytics off clears this analytics identity and stops new collection. It does not delete events already received by Amplitude; you can contact us about those records. Session replay and advertising pixels are not enabled by this website integration. Analytics providers receive network connection information when your browser contacts them.

App information is served by our own site function using Apple's public data, and images may load directly from Apple's servers, so Apple can receive your IP address. The site keeps a temporary list of apps in session storage, saves search state in browser history, and remembers your motion preference locally. Practice grading runs in your browser. If you allow optional analytics, the practice details described above are also sent to Amplitude; allowing analytics is not required to answer questions or see your results. The copy of this policy on qtech-dev.github.io is hosted by GitHub, which processes visitor connection data under its own policy.

12. Your choices and rights

Choices available to everyone. You can use the study features without using AI features, turn off reminders in iOS Settings, delete an App to remove its local data, and cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account. On neurontrainer.com, use Analytics settings to change your optional website analytics choice. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep optional Amplitude collection off; these controls do not change the separate Cloudflare page-view measurement.

Making a request. Email qtechdev1@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request" and the App name. For AI records, include the approximate date and time. Do not send sensitive content. Because the Apps have no accounts, we may be unable to connect a request to pseudonymous records without more information, and we may ask for details needed to verify the request. We respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. Providers' capabilities and legal or security retention requirements can limit what we can delete.

United States. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of your personal information, to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling, and to appeal our decision by replying to it. California residents may use an authorized agent. Section 9 explains what we do and do not sell or share.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland. You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a portable copy of your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Canada and Australia. You may request access to or correction of your personal information, withdraw consent subject to legal limits, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
